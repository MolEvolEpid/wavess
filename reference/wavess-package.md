# wavess: Within-Host Adaptive Virus Evolution Sequence Simulator

Simulates within-host virus sequence evolution. Optionally includes
recombination, a latent cell reservoir, and four types of selective
pressures (conserved sites, comparison to a "most fit" sequence, and
adaptive B-cell and T-cell immunity).
